# Privacy Policy

Unmark does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

- All image processing happens locally on your device.
- The app requests no network permission and makes no network requests.
- Photos you open are only read to display and edit them; edited results are only written
  back to your device's own photo gallery (`Pictures/Unmark`) when you tap Save.
- No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs are included.
- No account or sign-in is required or offered.
